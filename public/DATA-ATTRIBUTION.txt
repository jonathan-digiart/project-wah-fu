# Data attribution

Derived from the Lands Department, Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, 3D Visualisation Map (Non-textured models), available through the CSDI Portal and Open3Dhk.

Dataset: https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/dataset/landsd_rcd_1742809441342_98380
Source viewer: https://3d.map.gov.hk/
Terms: https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/doc/TNC

The Government and relevant organisations retain intellectual property rights in the source data. The source data and this project’s approximation are provided without a guarantee of accuracy. This is an independent, simplified reconstruction and is not endorsed by the Government. Building outlines and heights are simplified; terrain, ground contacts and access routes are provisional. Illustrative sea and presentation base are not surveyed bathymetry or geology.
