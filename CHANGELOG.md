## First public clay-model preview — 2026-11-03

[View the public preview](https://jonathan-digiart.github.io/project-wah-fu/) · [Published commit](https://github.com/jonathan-digiart/project-wah-fu/commit/348b19d7166fbee2ef01b6fb6bf6b048b3bf5e4b)

### Added

- An experimental browser-based procedural clay model of Wah Fu Estate and its surroundings, with generated terrain and simplified building forms.
- Orbit, pan, zoom and building focus controls, with a camera collision envelope.
- Source attribution and third-party notices for the published scene.

### Known limitations

- This is an early prototype, not a complete reconstruction or a survey. Terrain, building dimensions and building-to-ground connections remain provisional and require further review.
- Ground contacts, steps, access routes and walkability have not been verified. Performance on mobile devices also needs further review.

See [Development and release](docs/development-and-release.md) for further limitations and source information, and [Issues](https://github.com/jonathan-digiart/project-wah-fu/issues) for ongoing work.
