Project Wah Fu

An independent reconstruction of Wah Fu Estate, Hong Kong.

Data sources

Geometry and road reference data: Lands Department, Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, via the Common Spatial Data Infrastructure (CSDI) Portal and Hong Kong Map Service. Sources include 3D Visualisation Map (Non-textured models), iB1000 Digital Topographic Map, and True Digital Orthophoto (TDOP).

The Government and relevant organisations retain intellectual property rights in the source data. Data are available under the CSDI Portal terms and DATA.GOV.HK terms. This project is not endorsed by the Government.

Reading the model

Geometry includes modelled and inferred corrections and is not a survey. Road levels, boundaries, building details, and terrain transitions are approximate. The 25 m presentation base and sea extension are illustrative. Camera protection is experimental; orthographic inspection views have no physical camera collision. Source gaps and a short ribbed terrain transition near the northern bridge head remain.

All historical comparisons, private reference imagery and unused model variants are excluded from this public edition. The published model preserves the approved geometry; it is not a lossily simplified replacement.
